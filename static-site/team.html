Arif Hossain

Senior Video Editor

Dhaka-based editor with seven years behind the timeline, most of them spent cutting long-form for YouTube creators and course sellers. Arif takes raw footage and builds a story with a clear opening, a pace that holds attention past the ten-minute mark and an ending that lands, then hands the cut to colour and sound with clean, labelled project files. His strengths are story structure, pacing and dialogue-driven editing in Premiere Pro. Inside the studio he leads the first cut on every long-form project and mentors the junior editors on retention-focused pacing.

Skills & expertise
  • Story structure
  • Pacing & rhythm
  • Premiere Pro
  • Audio cleanup
  • Caption styling

Nusrat Jahan

YouTube Editor

Nusrat is the editor most clients talk to every week. She runs the upload cycle for creator channels — turning long interviews into watchable chapters, styling captions, cutting dead air and making sure each video is finished before the scheduled publish time without sanding off the creator's own tone. She works fast in Premiere Pro and is unusually good at reading a rough transcript to find the best twenty seconds. In the team she owns weekly delivery and coordinates the publish calendar with clients.

Skills & expertise
  • Interview editing
  • Caption styling
  • Chapter markers
  • Thumbnail stills
  • Workflow planning

Tanvir Ahmed

Motion Graphics Designer

Tanvir brings five years of After Effects work across brand films, ads and creator channels. He designs the animated layer of a video: logo reveals, lower thirds, stat callouts, transitions and the small motion details that make an edit feel expensive, all built around the client's type and colour system. His strengths are vector animation, kinetic type and clean transitions that never distract from the story. He owns the studio's motion template library, so every editor can drop in an intro that already looks on brand.

Skills & expertise
  • After Effects
  • Logo animation
  • Lower thirds
  • Vector illustration
  • Brand systems

Sadia Islam

Thumbnail Designer

Sadia designs the frame that decides whether a video gets watched at all. From a graphic design background with four years of YouTube thumbnails, she works one clear idea per image: high contrast, short readable type and a face expression that matches the promise of the title — always in two variants so clients can test the click. Her toolkit is Photoshop, composition and type hierarchy. She keeps every channel's thumbnails visually consistent and delivers platform-ready 1280×720 exports.

Skills & expertise
  • Photoshop
  • Composition
  • Typography
  • Face cut-outs
  • A/B variants

Rakib Hasan

Colourist & Sound Designer

Rakib is the last person to touch a project before it ships. With six years of grading and mixing, he fixes skin tones, balances exposure between shots, cleans dialogue down to the room tone and sits the music under the voice so nothing fights. He grades in DaVinci Resolve, mixes to platform loudness standards and prepares the final export settings. In the studio he owns the finishing pass, so every delivery leaves with a consistent look and a mix that survives phone speakers.

Skills & expertise
  • DaVinci Resolve
  • Colour grading
  • Noise removal
  • Audio mixing
  • Loudness standards

Mim Akter

Shorts & Reels Editor

Mim cuts the vertical side of the studio. She started as a creator editing her own Reels and now cuts Shorts, Reels and TikToks for brands and creators full time — hooks in the first two seconds, beat-matched cuts, captions that stay readable on a small screen and safe margins so nothing hides under the app UI. She is fast with vertical framing, hook variants and caption styling. She owns short-form delivery in the team and tests hooks before anything goes out.

Skills & expertise
  • Vertical editing
  • Hook writing
  • Beat sync
  • On-screen captions
  • 9:16 exports
