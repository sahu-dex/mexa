FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Our team primarily uses industry-standard software including Adobe Premiere Pro, After Effects, and DaVinci Resolve for color grading. For specific trendy short-form content, we also utilize CapCut Pro when necessary for specific effects.
Turnaround times vary based on complexity and length. Standard YouTube videos (10-15 mins) typically take 48-72 hours. Short-form content (TikTok/Reels) takes 24 hours. Complex commercial edits may take 5-7 business days.
We provide a secure, high-speed cloud portal (via Frame.io or Google Drive) where you can easily upload your raw footage and assets without size limitations.
We offer unlimited revisions on our Standard and Premium plans to ensure you are 100% satisfied. The Basic plan includes up to 2 rounds of revisions.
